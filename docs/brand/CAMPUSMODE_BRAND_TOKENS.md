# CampusMode Brand Tokens

Status: APPROVED SOURCE OF TRUTH

## Canonical identity
- Brand: CampusMode
- Tagline: Campus life, made easy.
- Wordmark typeface: Neue Machina
- Tagline typeface: Open Sans

## Core palette
- Campus Blue: #154597
- Mode Green: #20A953
- Midnight: #08121F
- Ink: #172033
- Cloud: #F1F3F6
- Warm Paper: #F0EDE8

## Product UI guidance
- Campus Blue is the primary trust/navigation colour.
- Mode Green represents active/positive CampusMode identity and "Mode ON" moments.
- Green must not be used to imply regulatory approval or verification unless the underlying status is actually approved.
- Midnight is the preferred dark-mode background family.
- Ink is the default readable text colour on light surfaces.
- Cloud/Warm Paper are neutral support surfaces.

## Logo typography rule
The CampusMode master logo is artwork, not live typography.
Do not rebuild the master logo by typing "CampusMode" in Neue Machina.
Use the approved artwork.

## Application typography
Neue Machina is a brand/display face.
Open Sans is the approved tagline and communications face.
Existing product UI typography may remain during Stage 1; any UI typography migration belongs to the visible product implementation stages and must be tested for layout/regression.

## Accessibility
Do not rely on colour alone for:
- verification
- approval
- suspension
- billing
- errors
- safety warnings

Use icon + text + accessible state labels.
