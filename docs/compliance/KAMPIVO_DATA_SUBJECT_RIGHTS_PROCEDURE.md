# Kampivo Data Subject Rights and Account Deletion Procedure

Owner: Privacy contact / designated DPO when appointed
Public contact: support@campuslink.name.ng (migrate during Stage D)
Target response: acknowledge promptly; respond within the period required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act/GAID. As an operational target, aim for 30 days.

## Supported requests

Access; rectification; erasure/account closure; restriction; objection; withdrawal of consent where applicable; portability where applicable; human review of qualifying automated decisions; complaint/escalation.

## Procedure

1. Log the request with date, channel, requester, account email and requested right.
2. Verify identity proportionately. Do not request more identity evidence than necessary.
3. Search the systems reasonably likely to contain the requester's data: Supabase Auth/database/storage, administration records, Resend communication records and billing/subscription records where relevant.
4. Check for lawful exceptions or holds: unresolved fraud/safety case, legal dispute, statutory accounting/tax retention, third-party rights.
5. Complete the request or explain the lawful reason for any limitation.
6. For erasure/account closure: disable access, remove public/profile visibility, delete data that has no continuing lawful purpose, schedule verification-document deletion, and preserve only the minimum records subject to a valid hold.
7. Record completion date, action taken and reviewer.
8. Tell the requester how to complain to the NDPC if dissatisfied.

## Security rule

Never send copies of another person's data in response to a request. Where a record contains mixed data, redact or separate third-party information before disclosure.
