# Kampivo Personal Data Breach Response Plan

Owner: Incident lead + privacy/DPO function
Escalation: Founder/management, engineering/security, privacy lead, relevant processor, legal/DPCO when necessary.

## First hour

1. Preserve evidence and open an incident record.
2. Contain the incident: revoke sessions/keys, isolate vulnerable functions, stop unsafe processing or sharing.
3. Identify systems, users, data categories and time window affected.
4. Do not destroy logs needed for investigation.

## Risk assessment

Assess confidentiality, integrity and availability impact; sensitivity and volume; ability to identify affected people; likelihood of fraud, identity theft, discrimination, financial loss, stalking or exposure of verification documents; and whether the data can be rendered inaccessible.

## Notification rule

Where a breach is likely to result in risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, Section 40 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires notification to the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware, where applicable. High-risk situations may also require prompt communication to affected data subjects.

Official breach channel: https://services.ndpc.gov.ng/breach/

## Required incident record

- Discovery and awareness timestamps
- Incident description and root cause
- Systems/data categories affected
- Approximate number of people/records
- Containment actions
- Risk assessment and decision on notification
- NDPC/data-subject communications
- Processor/vendor communications
- Remediation, lessons learned and follow-up owner

## After containment

Patch the cause, rotate affected credentials, validate access policies, restore safely, monitor for recurrence, update the DPIA/processor record/retention policy where needed, and conduct a post-incident review.
