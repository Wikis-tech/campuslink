# Kampivo Data Retention Schedule

Owner: Kampivo privacy/compliance lead
Last reviewed: 7 October 2026
Status: launch baseline; review at least every 6 months and after material product/legal changes.

## Principles

Kampivo retains personal data only while a defined business, safety, contractual or legal purpose remains. Deletion may be suspended by a documented legal hold, fraud investigation, active safety case, dispute or statutory record-keeping obligation.

## Operational schedule

| Record category | Default operational period | Disposal / review rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account/profile data | Active account + up to 24 months after closure | Delete/anonymise unless a continuing legal, fraud, safety or dispute purpose applies |
| Student/Vendor verification evidence | Until final verification decision + 180 days | Delete source evidence after appeal/safety window unless legal hold or unresolved case; retain minimal decision/audit metadata |
| Verification decision/audit metadata | Account life + up to 24 months after closure | Keep only the status, decision timestamp, reviewer/audit facts needed to evidence the decision |
| Marketplace saves/contact analytics | Up to 24 months | Aggregate/anonymise where longer trend analysis is useful |
| Notifications and routine communications | Up to 12 months after delivery | Delete or aggregate unless tied to an active case |
| Reviews and Vendor responses | While published + up to 24 months after removal/account closure | Retain longer only where needed for dispute/safety evidence |
| Complaints/safety cases | Resolution + up to 24 months | Extend for active disputes, repeat-abuse analysis, legal hold or authority request |
| Admin audit/security events | Up to 24 months | Longer retention only for an active security investigation/legal requirement |
| Billing/payment/subscription records | Statutory accounting/tax period | Do not store full payment-card data; follow applicable Nigerian record-keeping law |
| Backups | Provider lifecycle / rolling backup window | Ensure deleted primary data ages out through provider backup lifecycle |

## Required operating controls

- Run a quarterly retention review until automated retention jobs are implemented.
- Record legal holds and the reason/date/owner.
- Never keep raw verification evidence merely because storage is cheap.
- Deletion of source evidence must not erase the minimal audit proof needed to demonstrate a verification decision.
- Any retention change must be reflected in the public Privacy Policy where it materially changes user expectations.
